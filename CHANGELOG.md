# Changelog

The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/),
and this project adheres to [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

## [Unreleased]

### Added
- **T09 Signal framework & consent:** Privacy-safe `Copy` `WorkEvent` JSONL schema (`activity`, `git_commit`, `shell` with `deny_unknown_fields`) and lock-aware `EventSpool` (`append`, corrupt-line-skipping `read`, concurrent-tail-preserving `compact`, and `purge_signal`) in `crates/tg-store/src/spool.rs`.
- Tunable `[signals]` water rewards, shell minimum spacing, diminishing-returns half-life, and hourly/daily water caps in `data/balance.toml` (`SignalBalance` in `crates/tg-core/src/balance.rs`) plus persisted `WaterBudgetState` in `crates/tg-store/src/save.rs`.
- Shared consent disclosures in `data/consent.toml` (`ConsentCatalog`), opt-in collection gate (`is_signal_enabled`, `is_event_consented`, `record_if_consented`), and pure diminishing-returns/cap evaluator (`evaluate_work_events`) in `crates/tg-signals/src/{consent.rs,evaluator.rs}`.
- `niwa signals [list]`, `niwa signals enable <name> [--yes]`, and `niwa signals disable <name> [--purge]` CLI subcommands (`crates/niwa/src/cli.rs`), wired end-to-end into `GardenSession::open` and `GardenSession::advance_open_tui_ticks` (`crates/niwa/src/runtime.rs`) with diagnostic counter updates and spool compaction.
- Signal storage and local-only privacy specification in `docs/privacy.md`, verified by `proptest` spam/batching suites, `true`-loop vs honest-use checks, and CLI integration tests (`crates/tg-store/tests/spool_and_purge.rs`, `crates/tg-signals/tests/consent_and_caps.rs`, `crates/niwa/tests/signals_cli.rs`).
- **T10 Activity signal + daemon:** ADR `docs/decisions/003-macos-idle-time-source.md` choosing native `CoreGraphics` sampling in `Niwa.app` (T14) paired with a safe-Rust `/usr/sbin/ioreg` (`IOHIDSystem` `HIDIdleTime`) CLI fallback, plus verified platform specifications in `docs/platform/idle-time.md` and `docs/platform/daemon-units.md` (checked `2026-10-31`).
- Per-user `launchd` (`~/Library/LaunchAgents/io.github.quiinthieu.niwa.daemon.plist`, `ProcessType=Background`, `KeepAlive.SuccessfulExit=false`) and `systemd` (`${XDG_CONFIG_HOME:-~/.config}/systemd/user/niwa.service`, `Restart=on-failure`) unit renderer and atomic installer/uninstaller in `crates/tg-store/src/daemon_units.rs` with reference templates in `packaging/{launchd,systemd}/`.
- `IdleSource` trait, `FakeIdleSource`, `PlatformIdleSource` (macOS `ioreg` + Linux `org.gnome.Mutter.IdleMonitor`, `xprintidle`, and `org.freedesktop.ScreenSaver`), `any_signal_enabled`, and spool-deduping `sample_and_record_activity` in `crates/tg-signals/src/{activity.rs,consent.rs}`.
- `niwa daemon [run|install|uninstall|status]` (`--once`, `--max-ticks`) 60-second coalesced loop in `crates/niwa/src/{daemon.rs,cli.rs}` that skips waking/sleeping immediately when no signal is enabled, verified by `crates/tg-store/tests/daemon_units.rs`, `crates/tg-signals/tests/activity_idle.rs`, and `crates/niwa/tests/daemon_flow.rs`.
- **T11 Git signal:** Validated `git.repos` (order-preserving deduplication, control-character rejection, and `"none"` clearing) and `git.author_email` (`<email>` normalization, `local@domain` validation, and `"none"` clearing) in `crates/tg-store/src/config.rs`, plus persisted numeric-only `GitPollState` (`consent_anchor`, `last_poll_at`, `credited_commits`) and `GardenStore::clear_git_poll_state` in `crates/tg-store/src/save.rs` (documented in `docs/privacy.md`).
- Read-only `git --no-optional-locks log` poller (`ProcessGitLogRunner`, `CountingGitLogRunner`, `FakeGitLogRunner`, `expand_repo_path`, `parse_git_log_output`, and `poll_and_record_git_commits`) in `crates/tg-signals/src/git.rs` that matches `<author_email>` case-insensitively with `--fixed-strings`, queries local `--branches HEAD` (with `--branches` unborn-`HEAD` fallback), deduplicates across clones/re-polls and rewritten history (`git commit --amend` / `git rebase`), and throttles background polling to `GIT_POLL_INTERVAL_SECS` (`300 s`).
- End-to-end runtime, daemon, open-TUI, and `niwa signals disable git --purge` integration in `crates/niwa/src/{runtime.rs,daemon.rs,cli.rs}`, verified against throwaway temp git repositories and canary privacy audits in `crates/tg-signals/tests/git_polling.rs` and `crates/niwa/tests/git_signal_flow.rs`.
- **T12 Shell hook:** Verified `zsh` (`add-zsh-hook` `preexec`/`precmd`), `bash` (`DEBUG` trap + `PROMPT_COMMAND` for Bash `3.2–5.x`), and `fish` (`--on-event fish_postexec`) platform specification in `docs/platform/shell-hooks.md` (checked `2026-10-31`).
- Privacy-safe shell hook snippet renderer (`render_hook_snippet`), isolated rc file path resolver (`resolve_rc_path`), and byte-exact marked-block installer/uninstaller (`install_shell_hook`, `uninstall_shell_hook`) with `.niwa.bak` backups in `crates/tg-store/src/shell_hook.rs`, verified across boundary and `proptest` byte-for-byte restoration suites (`crates/tg-store/tests/shell_hooks.rs`, `crates/tg-signals/tests/shell_hook_props.rs`).
- `niwa hook zsh|bash|fish`, `niwa hook install [--write-rc]`, `niwa hook uninstall`, and lock-free `niwa _ingest --exit <code> --dur <secs>` fast path (`crates/niwa/src/{hook.rs,ingest.rs,cli.rs,main.rs}`), verified in real `zsh`, `bash`, and `fish` CI shells (`crates/niwa/tests/shell_hook_flow.rs`) with measured `--release` subprocess latency (`p50 = 2.643 ms`, `p95 = 4.079 ms`, target `< 10 ms`) recorded in `docs/perf.md`.
- **T13 Snapshot consumers:** Versioned `snapshot.json` (`schema_version = 1`) specification and ambient consumer guide in `docs/snapshot-schema.md`, `< 60 s` freshness predicate (`SNAPSHOT_MAX_AGE_SECS` and `Snapshot::is_fresh`) in `crates/tg-store/src/snapshot.rs`, `PromptStyle` `Display`/`FromStr` parsing in `crates/tg-store/src/config.rs`, and `stage_from_glyph` 1-cell glyph inverse in `crates/tg-render/src/glyph.rs`, verified by `insta` schema goldens and forward-compatible unknown-field decoding tests (`crates/tg-store/tests/snapshot_schema.rs`, `crates/tg-render/tests/sprites_and_glyphs.rs`).
- Lock-free `niwa prompt [--style glyph|emoji|none]` read-only fast path (`try_run_fast_prompt_from_env` and `run_prompt_with_writer`) with non-blocking stale-snapshot refresh (`GardenSession::try_refresh_snapshot_for_prompt`) plus `niwa prompt --tmux` and `niwa prompt --starship` snippet emitters (`crates/niwa/src/{prompt.rs,runtime.rs,cli.rs,main.rs}`), verified by `crates/niwa/tests/prompt_flow.rs` with measured `--release` subprocess latency (`p50 = 3.299 ms`, `p95 = 9.208 ms` default `glyph`; `p50 = 2.880 ms`, `p95 = 4.550 ms` `--style emoji`; `p50 = 2.389 ms`, `p95 = 4.071 ms` `--style none`; target `< 15 ms`) recorded in `docs/perf.md`.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-31

### Added
- **T01 Workspace & tooling:** Cargo workspace with `tg-core`, `tg-signals`, `tg-store`, `tg-render`, and `niwa-garden` (`niwa` binary) crate stubs.
- Single-source-of-truth product metadata in `crates/tg-core/src/product.rs`, wired into `niwa --version`.
- Workspace dependency-graph and allowed-dependency tests (`crates/niwa/tests/dependency_graph.rs`).
- Zero-network privacy lint (`scripts/lint-privacy.sh`) with planted-import integration tests (`crates/niwa/tests/privacy_lint.rs`).
- Toolchain pinning (`rust-toolchain.toml`), `rustfmt.toml`, `clippy.toml`, `deny.toml`, and GitHub Actions CI (`.github/workflows/ci.yml`) for Linux and macOS.
- **T02 Test harness & injection:** `Clock` trait, `Timestamp`, and `FakeClock` in `crates/tg-core/src/clock.rs`, plus `SystemClock` in `crates/tg-store/src/clock.rs`.
- Portable seeded PCG64 PRNG factory (`rng_from_seed`, `rng_for_tick`, `rng_for_stream`) in `crates/tg-core/src/rng.rs` and `insta` golden snapshot testing setup.
- `TestEnv` harness (isolated temporary `NIWA_HOME`, `HOME`, and XDG directories), `NiwaPaths` (`crates/tg-store/src/paths.rs`), and save/spool fixture loaders (`crates/tg-store/src/fixtures.rs`, `tests/fixtures/`).
- `tg-core` purity lint (`scripts/lint-core-purity.sh`) enforced in CI with planted-violation tests (`crates/niwa/tests/core_purity_lint.rs`).
- **T03 Core simulation:** Tunable gameplay balance parameters in `data/balance.toml` validated by `Balance` and `GrowthProfile` in `crates/tg-core/src/balance.rs`.
- Pure domain model (`Stage`, `Plant`, `Plot`, `SimError` in `crates/tg-core/src/model.rs`) and deterministic `Garden` simulation (`tick()`, `tick_n()`, `simulate_ticks()`, dormancy, and `(garden_seed, tick_index)` per-tick/per-plot seeding in `crates/tg-core/src/sim.rs`).
- Unit tests for every stage transition and dormancy cycle (`crates/tg-core/tests/sim_stages_and_dormancy.rs`) plus `proptest` suites for batching invariance, monotonicity, and non-loss (`crates/tg-core/tests/sim_properties.rs`).
- **T04 Species, rarity, seasons:** ADR `docs/decisions/001-date-crate-time.md` selecting `#![no_std]` `time` for deterministic UTC calendar season math, plus `Season`, `Hemisphere`, `SeasonMode`, and `resolve_season` in `crates/tg-core/src/season.rs`.
- Span-validated `data/species/*.toml` loader (`SpeciesCatalog`, `Species`, `Rarity`, `ColorHint`, and `SpeciesLoadError` reporting exact file/line/column in `crates/tg-core/src/species.rs`) with 16 initial species across all 4 rarities and 4 seasons, plus season-aware `Garden::tick_with_catalog`.
- Tunable seasonal and seed drop-table weights in `data/balance.toml` and deterministic `DropTable` sampler (`crates/tg-core/src/drops.rs`) with golden `insta` snapshot and hemisphere boundary tests (`crates/tg-core/tests/{seasons.rs,species_and_drops.rs}`).
- **T05 Store:** ADR `docs/decisions/002-file-lock-crate-fs4.md` selecting `fs4` for cross-platform advisory exclusive locks on `garden.lock` (`GardenLock` in `crates/tg-store/src/lock.rs`), plus macOS/Linux/`NIWA_HOME` path resolution (`NiwaPaths::resolve` and `PlatformKind` in `crates/tg-store/src/paths.rs`).
- Crash-safe atomic writer (`atomic_write_bytes` with fault injection in `crates/tg-store/src/atomic.rs`), `garden.json` v1 persistence with `.bak` rotation and recovery notices (`GardenStore`, `GardenSave`, `RecoveryNotice` in `crates/tg-store/src/save.rs`), and step-by-step save migration runner with `v0 -> v1` sample (`MigrationRunner`, `MigrateV0ToV1` in `crates/tg-store/src/migrate.rs`).
- Validated `config.toml` reader/writer covering all `PLAN.md` §6 settings (`AppConfig`, `ConfigStore` in `crates/tg-store/src/config.rs`) and versioned `snapshot.json` writer/reader tolerating unknown fields (`Snapshot`, `write_snapshot`, `read_snapshot` in `crates/tg-store/src/snapshot.rs`), verified by fault-injection, `.bak` recovery, `insta` golden, and multi-process concurrency tests (`crates/tg-store/tests/`).
- **T06 Offline catch-up:** Tunable `[catch_up]` parameters (`offline_cap_hours`, `offline_tail_factor`, `chunk_ticks`) in `data/balance.toml` and `CatchUpBalance` / `Balance::compute_tick_gp_with_base_scale` in `crates/tg-core/src/balance.rs`.
- Bounded-chunk offline catch-up engine (`Garden::catch_up`, `Garden::catch_up_with_events`, `CatchUpOutcome`) with backwards clock-skew protection (`ClockSkewNote`) and diagnostic counter integration (`GardenSave::record_catch_up` in `crates/tg-store/src/save.rs`), plus cozy "While you were away" summaries (`AwaySummary` in `crates/tg-core/src/catch_up.rs`).
- 30-day batching-invariance unit + `proptest` suites, §7 `< 200 ms` 30-day benchmark check, and `insta` golden summary snapshot (`crates/tg-core/tests/catch_up.rs`).
- **T07 Renderer:** Multi-line Unicode and 7-bit ASCII stage sprite bank (`data/sprites/{unicode,ascii}/*.toml` for all 16 species across all 5 stages, validated by `SpriteBank` and `SpriteLoadError` in `crates/tg-render/src/sprites.rs`) plus shared `SpriteSet` in `crates/tg-core/src/species.rs`.
- Terminal color tiers (`NoColor`, `Ansi16`, `Ansi256`, `Truecolor`), pure `NO_COLOR` / `--no-color` capability detection (`detect_color_tier` in `crates/tg-render/src/color.rs`), seasonal palettes (`SeasonPalette` and `plant_style` in `crates/tg-render/src/palette.rs`), and 1-cell compact glyph (`compact_glyph` in `crates/tg-render/src/glyph.rs`).
- Wide-character-safe terminal display-width helpers (`crates/tg-render/src/width.rs`), responsive grid renderer (`>= 40x12`) with compact list fallback (`< 40x12`) and non-color state labels (`render_garden` and `render_compact_frame` in `crates/tg-render/src/layout.rs`), verified by `proptest` width invariants and 26 `insta` golden snapshots across `40x12`, `80x24`, `120x40`, and `32x10` (`crates/tg-render/tests/`).
- **T08 TUI & CLI (MVP release):** Interactive `ratatui` + `crossterm` TUI (`niwa`) with responsive grid/compact views, plot cursor & non-color inspector bar, Seed Pouch planting (`p`), blooming harvest (`h`), plant move (`m`), same-rarity compost (`c`), Almanac screen (`a`, rarity labels and bloom dates without drop odds), Help overlay (`?`), "While you were away" offline catch-up modal, and active-signal `sun` ticks while open (`crates/niwa/src/tui/`).
- Power-budget `FrameScheduler` (`crates/niwa/src/tui/scheduler.rs`) capping active redraws at `config.fps` (`1..=4` fps) and dropping to `0` fps after `30 s` without input or on `FocusLost`, sleeping until the next `60 s` simulation tick.
- CLI subcommands (`crates/niwa/src/{cli.rs,runtime.rs}`) for `niwa peek` (`--width`, `--height`, `--ascii`), `niwa status [--json]`, `niwa config get|set|list|path`, and `niwa reset [--yes] [--all]` (via `GardenStore::reset` in `crates/tg-store/src/save.rs`), plus global `--no-color` support, verified by `assert_cmd` and `TestBackend` integration suites (`crates/niwa/tests/{cli_subcommands.rs,tui_flow.rs}`).
